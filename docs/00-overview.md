# Overview

Atmos Real Estate is a marketplace, a developer directory, and an editorial site
for the Albanian property market, in Albanian, English and Italian.

## The four pillars (client vision doc, §29)

| Pillar | What it means | Phase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketplace | Properties, projects, units, search, map | v1 |
| Matchmaking | Client criteria matched to new listings automatically | v2 |
| Intelligence | Price data, area guides, comparables | v3 |
| Media | Market news linked to properties, projects and companies | v3 |

v1 builds the marketplace only. The other three depend on having real inventory
and traffic first.

## Who uses it

- **Client** — searches, saves favourites, contacts an agent. Registration optional in v1.
- **Owner** — submits a property through a private form. Cannot publish directly.
- **Company / developer** — has a public profile page and projects. Managed by Atmos in v1.
- **Agent** — Atmos staff, manages listings and enquiries in the Payload admin.
- **Admin** — full access, approves everything.

## The rule that shapes the whole product

Owners do not publish. They submit a request; Atmos verifies it and creates the
listing. This is the client's core positioning — quality control is the product.

It means the public site has almost no write operations in v1: an enquiry form
and a property submission form, both landing in an admin queue.
